# [Sonnet 5.5] ARC-10 — quiver on Scarb 2.20.1

Model: Sonnet 5.5 (`claude-sonnet-5-5`), as the brief says.

## Summary

The workspace builds, tests, measures and packages with **Scarb 2.20.1 (Cairo 2.20.0) and
starknet-foundry 0.64.0**. Every test of both packages passes (quest 512, achievement 145) and every
gas figure was re-measured. Pull request: https://github.com/bal7hazar/quiver/pull/37 (CI green).
Library code is unchanged: the only edits under `packages/*/src` are `#[available_gas]` budgets and
their notes in test modules. The single-thread pin (`RAYON_NUM_THREADS=1`) is **kept**, as SPK-13
says, and left as ARC-09 wrote it.

**The finding that drives everything else: snforge 0.64 charges 15 000 L2 gas more per storage write
and 6 000 more per storage read** (created slot 474 106, was 459 106; overwritten 72 106, was 57 106;
read 36 205, was 30 205), and every test has an 8 030 lower fixed overhead. No worst call exceeds the
20 M cap (worst: 15 666 913, 78 %).

## What broke and how it was fixed

1. **30 quest tests exceeded their budget** (their cost rose beyond the 5 % margin): raised to
   `ceil(1.05 × measured)` with `// gas: raised, D-180 Scarb 2.20.1` (9 in `test_component_probe`, 18 in
   `test_component_reentry`, 3 in `test_component_bench`). The note sits above the attributes, the
   place `scripts/gas.py` can parse (a comment between two attributes hides the test from it; found
   and fixed).
2. **The guards of the writes** (`test_component_writes`, 7 tests): a second write in a call costs
   **73 820** Sierra gas, not 58 820 (measured with a temporary `println!`, removed). The test
   `write_costs_58_820_sierra_gas` is renamed `write_costs_73_820_sierra_gas`; the six references
   moved from 802 776, 639 896, 680 079, 760 992, 654 102, 772 245 to 890 346, 699 606, 739 789,
   842 562, 713 812, 847 765, taken from the failing assertions' own messages. The tolerance (20 000)
   is kept: a write added to a call still moves it by 73 820. The comment in achievement's
   `achievement_progress_writes_nothing` gives the new unit; the test passed unchanged.
3. **Budgets far above their measure**: 192 quest and 66 achievement budgets were lowered to
   `ceil(1.05 × measured)`, as `gas.py --check` requires (all tests moved).
4. Nothing else broke: no change of library code, no `edition` change.

## Deviations from the brief

- `cairo-version`/`starknet` are `2.20.0`, not `2.20.1`: that is the Cairo Scarb 2.20.1 ships.
- **Not re-derived, left as records of their step** (each marked as measured on Scarb 2.19.4 /
  snforge 0.61): `GAS.md`'s "Where the tests went", the ARC-06 mechanism, 0.1.0's cost model and the
  fix loops, and in `docs/BUDGETS.md` the ARC-06 store paragraph (the `get`/`set` unit costs). Their
  numbers were taken at a past commit and several were measured by probes no longer in the tests. The
  cap and entrypoint tables, which carry the current figures, are all re-derived.
- The 0.2.0 tables' *Difference* column compares 0.2.0 on 2.20.1 with a *Before* of 2.19.4, so it now
  includes the compiler/snforge effect; the headers say so.
- The network estimate is `snforge − 20 606 per created slot − 40 106 per overwritten slot` (was
  5 606 / 25 106): the network's own price (453 500 / 32 000, FND-04) is unchanged, snforge's rose by 15 000.
- `.github/workflows/*.yml` not edited: the pins are read from `.tool-versions`.
- I used a scratchpad script to rewrite budgets and the tables of the docs; its results were checked
  with `gas.py --check` and spot-checked against the measures.
